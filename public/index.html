What are the Terms & Privacy Policy?

Effective Date: June 2026

Terms of Service

1. Acceptance of Terms

By registering as a rider on Road Warrior Pro, you agree to abide by our operational guidelines, referral program rules, and community standards.

2. Rider Responsibilities

You agree to provide accurate information during registration, maintain your vehicle in safe condition, and adhere to local traffic laws.

3. Referral Rewards

Milestone points and referral rewards are subject to verification. We reserve the right to withhold rewards if fraudulent activity is detected.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

1. Data Collection

We collect personal information necessary to provide our services, including your name, email, phone number, vehicle details, and location.

2. Rider Registration

Information collected during registration is used to set up your profile, verify your identity, and facilitate earnings tracking via our unified dashboard.

3. Referral Tracking

We use your data and referral codes to accurately track referrals and award milestone bonuses.

4. Analytics Usage

We use Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, and Meta Pixel to track website usage, understand behavior, and improve our services.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions, you can contact us at contact@roadwarriorev.com or call +91 6360 483 386.
