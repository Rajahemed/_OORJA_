Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection

We collect essential information to provide a seamless experience on Road Warrior Pro. This includes your name, contact details, vehicle information, and delivery platform data. All data is encrypted and securely stored.

2. Rider Registration

When you register as a rider, we store your profile data to enable access to the Rider Dashboard, track your EV transition progress, and manage your insurance preferences.

3. Referral Tracking

Our referral system uses unique codes to track invitations. When someone registers using your code, both parties receive reward points. We securely log these transactions to ensure accurate point distribution.

4. Analytics Usage

We use third-party tools like Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, and Meta Pixel to understand how users interact with our platform. This helps us optimize the user experience and improve our services. These tools may use cookies to track anonymous usage data.
